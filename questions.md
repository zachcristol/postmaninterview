# Questions to Ask — Postman

1. Walk me through a standard incident response. Has the culture ever felt accusatory rather than constructive?
2. Are there technical mistakes that are actively being rectified, or ones that still need to be?
3. How does Postman think about success metrics? At Block, teams shifted to heavily indexing PR acceptance count. When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure. How does Postman handle that?
4. What's the magnitude and frequency of org thrash — teams forming and dissolving, people and products moving between teams?
5. What does the on-call situation look like? Are wakeups tracked and worked down, or is a certain volume just accepted?
6. How much ownership does an individual engineer have over a system end-to-end — do you build it, deploy it, monitor it?
7. What does a strong first six months look like for someone joining this team?
8. When someone has a legitimate complaint about working here, what do you hear coming up most often?
9. What's something about working here that surprised you — that you didn't expect before joining?
